Concept cards

  • p-value: the probability of observing a result at least as extreme as the current one if there were truly no difference; it is not an effect size and not the probability that the hypothesis is true.
  • Adjusted p-value (FDR/padj): controls the false discovery rate when thousands of genes are tested at once; for example padj < 0.05 means about 5% of the features called significant are expected to be false discoveries.
  • log2 fold change: the base-2 logarithm of the ratio of expression between two groups; 1 means about 2-fold higher, -1 means about half. The sign depends on the comparison order.
