!Series_title = Genome-wide transcriptional targets of KLF15 in human airway smooth muscle

!Series_pubmed_id = 28375666
!Series_summary = We previously demonstrated that the transcription factor, KLF15, is a glucocorticoid-regulated gene that represses primary human airway smooth muscle (ASM) proliferation. Here, we show that KLF15 also represses ASM hypertrophy. To uncover the mechanistic basis for these effects, we integrated transcriptome data from KLF15 over-expression with genome-wide analysis of RNA Polymerase II (RNAPII) and glucocorticoid receptor (GR) occupancy (i.e. ChIP-seq). This led us to identify PLCD1 as both a KLF15-regulated gene and a repressor of ASM hypertrophy.
!Series_overall_design = Genome-wide transcriptional targets of KLF15 in human airway smooth muscle cells were determined by RNA-seq in cells transduced with a KLF15-expressing adenovirus (Ad-KLF15) compared to a control adenovirus (Ad-GFP).

!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = A modified protocol was used with the KAPA Stranded mRNA-Seq Kit (KAPA Biosystems) to construct sequencing libraries from 100 ng of isolated total RNA. Libraries were sequenced as barcoded-pooled samples on a P1 Ion Proton chip/Ion Proton NGS platform (Life Technologies).
!Sample_data_processing = Basecalling, barcode demultiplexing and adapter trimming was performed by Ion Torrent Suite 4.0.2.
!Sample_data_processing = Sequence reads of at least 30 nt length were mapped to the UCSC release hg19 of the human genome using GSNAP version 2013-11-27 with SNP data version 137 and splice sites compatible with the Ensembl annotation, as well as detection of novel splice sites.
!Sample_data_processing = Reads mapping unambiguously to each gene in the Ensembl annotation (downloaded from the UCSC genome browser July 29, 2013) were quantified using the htseq-count program from the HTSeq package (version 0.6.0) in the “intersection-nonempty” mode.
!Sample_data_processing = Statistical comparisons of the gene expression between the cell genotypes were performed with the DESeq2 package version 1.4.5 for the R statistical software version 3.1.0.
