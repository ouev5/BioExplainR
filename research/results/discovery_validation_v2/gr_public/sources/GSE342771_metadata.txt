!Series_title = Post-transcriptional glucocorticoid receptor signalling synchronises circadian rhythms (human TT-seq)
!Series_geo_accession = GSE342771
!Series_status = Public on Sep 08 2026
!Series_submission_date = Aug 06 2026
!Series_last_update_date = Sep 09 2026
!Series_summary = Circadian rhythms are driven by cellular clocks throughout the body that maintain optimal health. Glucocorticoid (GC) hormones are vital for synchronising cellular clocks, aligning them with external day/night cycles, but mechanistic understanding is incomplete. We use a glucocorticoid receptor KO/rescue approach to show that residue E755 and the N-terminal intrinsically disordered region are essential for GC synchronisation, whereas nuclear translocation from the cytoplasm is not. Using nascent and mature transcriptomics with (phospho)proteomics we show that, under physiological GC increases, the GR regulates a substantial proportion of gene expression post-transcriptionally. Rapid upregulation of Per1 mRNA occurs through increased RNA processing not synthesis, which drives GC synchronisation via acute increases in nascent PER1 and not PER2 protein. We find synchronisation to a single GC dose is highly heterogeneous between individual cells. Our findings provide mechanistic insight into nuclear hormone receptor signalling and establish a cellular basis for the gradual alignment of circadian clocks by daily environmental cycles via systemic cues.
!Series_overall_design = TT-seq was performed on RNA samples from U2OS Flp-In T-REx GR-knockout cells rescued with wild-type GR alpha, GR E755R (AF2), or GR IDR delta1-398 (IDR398). Cells were treated with DMSO or CORT for 90 min, with 4-thiouridine added for the final 30 min of treatment. RNA was then collected for parallel TT-seq and mRNA-seq experiments.

!Sample_treatment_protocol_ch1 = DMSO vehicle or 100 nM corticosterone was added as indicated. After 1 h at 37°C, 4-thiouridine was added to 1 mM and cells were incubated for exactly 30 min before RNA collection.
!Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = Human U2OS Flp-In T-REx GR-knockout rescue lines were cultured in high-glucose DMEM containing 10% HyClone FetalClone II bovine serum and penicillin/streptomycin at 37°C with 5% CO₂. Flp-In T-REx lines were maintained with 50 ug/mL hygromycin and GR transgene expression was induced with 1 ug/mL doxycycline for at least 24 h. For RNA sequencing, cells were seeded at high density in 15 cm dishes, grown to confluence, entrained for 4-7 days using alternating cycles of 12 h at 37°C and 12 h at 32°C, then incubated at constant 37°C for at least 24 h before sampling.
!Sample_molecule_ch1 = other
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = 3 mL TRIzol was added to cells, and the resulting lysates were snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -70°C. Total RNA was extracted by phenol-chloroform precipitation and quantified by Qubit. A total of 55 µg human RNA was supplemented with 550 ng 4SU-labelled S. cerevisiae spike-in RNA, fragmented using NaOH, biotinylated, and purified using streptavidin beads.
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = TT-seq libraries were constructed from 42 ng purified nascent RNA using the KAPA RNA HyperPrep Kit. Libraries were quality controlled using TapeStation high-sensitivity D5000 tapes, quantified by Qubit, pooled and sequenced in-house on a NextSeq 2000 with paired-end 60 bp reads.
!Sample_description = Library name: TT_human_alpha_CORT_rep1
!Sample_description = Biological replicate 1. Matched assay sample: mRNA_human_alpha_CORT_rep1. 4SU-labelled nascent RNA isolated by biotinylation and streptavidin purification. The processed count file contains gene-level unnormalised counts.
!Sample_data_processing = Reads were adapter-trimmed and quality-filtered using Trim Galore v0.6.7 with a Phred quality cutoff of 30.
!Sample_data_processing = Trimmed reads were aligned in two-pass mode to hg38 using STAR v2.7.9a. Multimapping reads were randomly assigned and restricted to a single alignment.
!Sample_data_processing = Alignments were sorted and indexed with SAMtools v1.9, then PCR duplicates were removed using GATK MarkDuplicates v4.3.0.0.
!Sample_data_processing = Gene-level raw counts were quantified using HTSeq v2.0.5 against Ensembl GRCh38 release 102; all reads overlapping annotated gene bodies were counted.
!Sample_data_processing = Read pairs were separated into strand-specific BAMs, according to whether they aligned to the plus or minus genomic strand, using SAMtools flag filters. Replicate-level 10-bp bigWigs were generated from the resulting strand-specific BAMs using deepTools bamCoverage with DESeq2-derived scale factors. For each condition and strand, normalised replicate bigWigs were summed with UCSC bigWigMerge, the resulting bedGraph signal was divided by the number of summed replicates to obtain the arithmetic mean, coordinates were sorted and restricted to the hg38 chromosome sizes, and the final bigWig was generated with UCSC bedGraphToBigWig.
!Sample_data_processing = Assembly: GRCh38/hg38
!Sample_data_processing = Supplementary files format and content: .txt files: tab-delimited gene-level unnormalised raw read counts from HTSeq v2.0.5; all reads overlapping annotated gene bodies were counted.
!Sample_data_processing = Supplementary files format and content: .bw files: condition-level arithmetic-mean coverage tracks at 10-bp resolution, provided separately for reads aligning to the plus or minus genomic strand. Replicate tracks were scaled using DESeq2-derived factors before averaging.
