Raw file names have been manually adjusted to meet the standart Patient ID encription.


LFQ-intensity based sample names are encripted as follows:

..._co1 = ...control 1
..._dex1 = ...dexamethasone 1 

Per patient there are 3 control and 3 dexamethasone samples (technical replicates)
Raw files are encripted as 6-digit codes (Patient IDs, date of tissue biopsy retieval) followed by coX or dexX (see above).
